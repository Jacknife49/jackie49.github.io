Hi, I am **Uddalak Mukherjee**.

I am a first-year PhD student at **Université Paris Dauphine–PSL**, working under the supervision of **Edwige Cyffers** and **Yann Chevaleyre** since September 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of **performative learning**, **reinforcement learning**, and **long-term trustworthy machine learning**. I study how algorithms influence the environments in which they operate, and how the resulting feedback can affect their fairness over time.

In particular, I am interested in bridging long-term fairness and performative prediction through **performative reinforcement learning**, with performativity modeled using infinite-horizon discounted Markov decision processes. My doctoral topic also explores connections to differential privacy and other safeguards for trustworthy machine learning.

My mathematical interests include:

- Measure-theoretic probability
- Convex geometry and optimization
- Functional analysis
- Statistical learning theory, online learning, and sequential decision-making

Before my PhD, I was a Project Research Fellow at **ACMU, Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata**, where I began a parallel line of research on **graph property testing**. I completed my master's studies at **RKMVERI**, with a thesis supervised by **Debabrota Basu** at **Inria, Lille**.
